Campaign Page Speed Review

In Phase 1, our changes took the key pages of vestwell.com from a median mobile score of 52 to 77. We can now do the same for the campaign pages on save.with.vestwell.com. We tested all 162 live pages on Google PageSpeed Insights, three runs each on mobile and desktop, and used the median result throughout. Below is where these pages stand today, what is holding them back, and how much each of our changes can improve them.

What is holding the pages back

Four issues account for nearly all of the lost points. Each one comes with a clear fix that we can make inside the HubSpot templates, without changing the design, content or tracking of the pages.

01

The main image appears late

On phones, the median page takes 7.4 seconds to show its main content, mostly because the hero image is large and loads late.

Our fix: compress and correctly size the hero image, and load it before anything else on the page.

Affects 139 of 162 pages on mobile
Mobile +9Desktop, minimal
02

Scripts keep the page busy

Scripts block the page for 670ms on mobile and 483ms on desktop, so visitors wait before they can scroll or click.

Our fix: load non-essential scripts after the page is ready. All tracking stays exactly as it is today.

Affects 110 pages on mobile and 142 on desktop
Mobile +5Desktop +7
03

The first screen waits on styles and code

On phones, the first content takes 2.8 seconds to appear because the page waits for its styles and scripts to finish.

Our fix: load only what the first screen needs, and defer the rest.

Affects 71 pages on mobile
Mobile +3Desktop, minimal
04

The layout moves while loading

Images, embeds and banners push content around as they load, which is distracting and can cause mis-clicks.

Our fix: reserve space for these elements in advance.

Affects 81 pages on mobile and 64 on desktop
Mobile +1Desktop +1

Also worth noting

From the same review

Pages not scored: of the 179 URLs checked, 15 redirect to the main site and 2 return "page not found" (/intuit-quickbooks-b and /intuit-quickbooks-c). We have noted these separately.

Search basics: none of the 162 pages are listed in a sitemap, 158 have no meta description, and none have a social share image. These are quick fixes we can include.

HubSpot limits: HubSpot's own scripts cannot be removed, so around 47 pages may stay below 65 on mobile. We will flag these individually after the changes.

How we measured
  1. Tool: Google PageSpeed Insights, mobile and desktop, three runs per page. The median run is used for every figure.
  2. Expected scores: our estimates, based on how much each issue costs under Google's own scoring. The gains overlap, so they do not add up exactly.
  3. Verification: every page will be re-tested the same way after the changes, with a before and after report.
  4. No change to tracking: all analytics, ad and HubSpot tracking stays exactly as it is today.
