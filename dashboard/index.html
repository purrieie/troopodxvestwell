What we need from you

Everything in one go, so we can start without back-and-forth. We prepare every change; your team reviews and publishes.

Access

  • HubSpotPermission to edit reports and dashboards, properties, workflows, tracking settings, custom events and the Salesforce connection settings.
  • SalesforceA read-only user, to check the call and meeting fields we bring into HubSpot.
  • LeanDataView access to BookIt, or confirmation that its meetings are readable in Salesforce.
  • Google Tag ManagerEdit access. We set up the changes in a workspace, and your team reviews and publishes them.

  • SemrushView access, to track the SEO and AI visibility results from phase 3.
